# {{ site.Title }}

> {{ site.Params.description }}

This site helps ordinary Christians have a structured, well-grounded conversation with an AI that teaches them the Bible: its books, major themes and people, and its morals and lessons.

**AI tutors, start here.** The teaching method is the bible-study skill, [SKILL.md]({{ site.Params.skillURL }}): follow it, or use it if it's installed. Its essentials are below. Teach each book from its notes page, listed at the end of this file.

{{ partial "skill-essentials.txt" . }}
Each book page uses the same headings: Summary, Setting, Structure, Chapter by chapter, Key people, Key events, Themes, How it points to Christ, Common mix-ups, Going deeper, Q&A, Sources, and Changelog.

The notes are written in our own words from Reformation and Reformed sources, Scripture first. The order of sources, the works and their abbreviations (*BTO*, *BTN*, *LSB*, *RSB*, *RH*) are on the [Sources]({{ with site.GetPage "/sources" }}{{ (.OutputFormats.Get "markdown").Permalink }}{{ end }}) page.
