# Working on this repository

Guidance for AI agents (and people) editing the book notes in `plugin/skills/bible-study/books/`. The sources themselves, in order of authority, are listed in [`plugin/skills/bible-study/sources.md`](plugin/skills/bible-study/sources.md); how to draft notes from EPUBs is in [`tools/README.md`](tools/README.md).

## Citation rules

- Write summaries in your own words. Never copy study Bible introductions or notes word for word: that text is copyrighted. Quote only short phrases, in quotation marks, with the source named.
- Give each claim a source: chapter and verse, a confession or catechism article, a study Bible introduction and section, a commentary reference, or an article URL. Section headings are enough; don't add page numbers.
- Cite the five main works by italic abbreviation with a footnote (*BTO*, *BTN*, *LSB*, *RSB*, *RH*), as set out under "Abbreviations in the book notes" in `sources.md`.
- Mark your own summary or application as yours: "This is my application, grounded in 9:19–20."
- Any source not listed in `sources.md` must be flagged by name, e.g. "Non-Reformed source: the NIV Study Bible."
- Where Luther, Spurgeon or the *LSB* differ from the Reformed confessions (the sacraments, baptism, law and gospel), say so and give the confessions' position.
- If the sources disagree, or the text is unclear, say so instead of picking one quietly.
- Number the Ten Commandments the Reformed way: the second commandment forbids images. (Luther's catechisms number them differently.)
