# Book notes checklist

Drafting every book's notes from the indexed sources, in canonical order. Each book gets all template sections, including "Chapter by chapter" and "Going deeper", and is set to `status: draft` (awaiting human review).

Sources used for every book:

- **Old Testament:** the chapter on the book in Van Pelt, ed., *A Biblical-Theological Introduction to the Old Testament* (each chapter has its own author), and the *Lutheran Study Bible* introduction.
- **New Testament:** the chapter on the book in Kruger, ed., *A Biblical-Theological Introduction to the New Testament*, and the *Lutheran Study Bible* introduction.

Shared source chapters (read once, used for several books): Van Pelt's "1–2 Samuel", "1–2 Kings", "1–2 Chronicles", "Ezra–Nehemiah" and "The Twelve" (all twelve minor prophets); Kruger's "1–2 Corinthians", "1–3 John" and "Introduction to the Pastoral Epistles".
