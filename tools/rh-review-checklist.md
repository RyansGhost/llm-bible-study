# Reformation Heritage KJV Study Bible review checklist

Each book's notes checked against *The Reformation Heritage KJV Study Bible* (general editor Joel R. Beeke): its introduction to the book (authorship, date, theme, purpose, synopsis, and outline where given) and the one-line summary at the head of each chapter's notes. Summary, Setting, Structure, Chapter by chapter, Key people, Key events, Themes and How it points to Christ are corrected or sharpened where it adds to or differs from them, in our own words and keeping each note about the same length. Each revised book cites the introduction's sections and the chapter summaries in its Sources.

The EPUB is indexed with `tools/epub_index.py` (work `beeke-reformation-heritage-kjv-study`). The EPUB itself is never committed.
