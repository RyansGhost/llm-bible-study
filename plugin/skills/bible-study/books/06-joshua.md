# Joshua

## Sources

- Reformation Study Bible, introduction to Joshua (to verify)
- Ligonier devotional on Joshua's courage: <https://learn.ligonier.org/devotionals/joshua-18>
- Matthew Henry on Joshua 9 (oath to the Gibeonites)

## Summary

God keeps his promise to Abraham by bringing Israel into the land under Joshua, though the conquest is left incomplete.

## Setting

Right after Moses' death; Israel camped east of the Jordan, then in Canaan.

## Structure

- 1–12: entering and conquering the land
- 13–21: dividing the land among the tribes
- 22–24: final warnings and covenant renewal

## Key people

- Joshua: Moses' successor, faithful spy, told to be strong and courageous
- Rahab: Canaanite in Jericho who hides the spies; saved by faith; in Jesus' line
- Achan: Israelite who steals from Jericho and brings defeat at Ai
- Caleb: the other faithful spy; claims Hebron at 85
- The Gibeonites: trick Israel into a treaty

## Key events

- Rahab hides the spies; the scarlet cord (2)
- Crossing the Jordan on dry ground (3–4)
- The walls of Jericho fall (6)
- Achan's sin and the defeat at Ai (7); Ai taken (8)
- The Gibeonite treaty (9)
- The sun stands still at Gibeon (10)
- Caleb receives Hebron (14)
- "As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord" (24:15)

## Themes

- God fights for his people (10:14)
- Faith versus unbelief: Rahab versus Achan
- Keep your word, even at a cost (9:19–20; 2 Sam 21; Ps 15:4). This is an application, grounded in those texts, not a source's words.
- Seek God before deciding (9:14)

## How it points to Christ

Joshua and Jesus share a name meaning "the Lord saves"; Hebrews 4 says Joshua's rest was not the final rest. Rahab appears in Matthew 1 and Hebrews 11.

## Common mix-ups

- The conquest is not complete; that sets up Judges.
- Moses' death is in Deuteronomy, not Joshua.

## Q&A

1. Who hid the spies in Jericho? Rahab.
2. Why did Israel lose at Ai? Achan kept loot from Jericho.
3. How did the Gibeonites trick Israel? Worn-out clothes and mouldy bread, posing as a distant people.
4. What happened at Gibeon? The sun stood still until the battle was won.
5. What did Joshua say in his farewell? "As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord."
